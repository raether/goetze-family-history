# Bernhard Götze

*Glauchau and Berlin*

Bernhard was [Rudolf Götze’s](rudolf-goetze.md) younger brother. Gutrune says that he inherited their father’s factory, sold it to pay his siblings their shares, and went into wholesale trading for textile mills. She remembers him living in Berlin and later training her brother Hermann as a textile sales representative.

Bernhard married [Johanna Römmler](johanna-roemmler.md), the younger sister of Rudolf’s wife Else. The two couples’ children were therefore first cousins through both families. Gutrune names Bernhard and Johanna’s daughters as [Hildegard](hildegard-goetze.md), [Erna](erna-goetze.md), and [Brigitte](brigitte-goetze.md).
