# Emil Hermann Götze

*1827–1902*

Emil Hermann Götze was a textile manufacturer in Glauchau, Saxony. An 1856 industry publication names Emil Herrmann Götze and Carl Eduard Götze as owners of *Götze senior und Söhne*. Gutrune Falckon, his granddaughter, recalled that Emil Hermann became wealthy through the use of power looms.

He married [Albertine Friedericke Caroline Brunner](friedericke-brunner.md) in Frankfurt am Main in 1857. The Dresden marriage register identifies them as [Rudolf’s](rudolf-goetze.md) parents. Gutrune names four surviving children: Frieda, Bruno, Rudolf, and [Bernhard](bernhard-goetze.md).

Gutrune later received a family account of a possible New York branch of the textile business. She proposed Emil Hermann as one possible founder if it opened later than the suggested date of about 1810. The date, founder, and existence of the branch remain open questions.
