# Raimund Götze

*1899–1990*

[Biography index](../README.md)

## Biography

### Childhood and studies in Germany

Raimund Konrad Ferdinand Götze was born on 8 October 1899 in Naunhof near Leipzig. A University Library of Rostock catalogue gives the Naunhof civil register reference as **93/1899**. He grew up in Heppenheim, where his sister Gutrune remembered climbing the Starkenburg ruins with him and using a walnut tree as a place to do their schoolwork. She added in the margin of her chronicle that he helped her with algebra and geometry. [Rostock bookplate catalogue, pp. 64–65][rostock] · [Gutrune’s chronicle][chronicle]

Gutrune recalls that Raimund studied at Tübingen and Göttingen. His 1921 Tübingen dissertation, *Liniengruppen und innere Quanten*, concerned the theory of spectral lines. The following year, Friedrich Paschen and R. Götze published a reference work on spectral series, which the Torelló historian Santi Molera i Clota attributes to Paschen and Raimund. Gutrune recalls a research post in Frankfurt, and Molera i Clota reports that Raimund worked at the Einstein Tower in Potsdam in the late 1920s. [Tübingen dissertation listing][theses] · [Springer book record][springer] · [Torelló history][torello] · [Gutrune’s chronicle][chronicle]

### Teaching and photography in Catalonia

By 1931, Raimund was teaching at the Aurora workers’ school in Torelló. He offered private lessons and helped run Catalan grammar courses in 1932 and 1935. He also took photographs professionally, taught darkroom work to local photographer Ramon Vinyeta, and organized a photography exhibition in 1933. He wrote about science for the local newspaper *La Veu del Ter* and published *Elementos de física matemática* with Editorial Labor in 1936. [Torelló history][torello]

### Marriage and flight from Spain

Molera i Clota dates Raimund’s marriage to Marie Louise Lagrange at the Torelló justice of the peace court to **16 September 1934** and the birth of their twins, Yvonne and Rolanda, to **14 April 1935**. Gutrune gives the same wedding day and month with the year **1943**. The 1934 date fits her account of the family leaving Spain with the twins during the Civil War. The original marriage entry would confirm the date. [Torelló history][torello] · [Gutrune’s chronicle][chronicle]

In 1936 the family moved to Manlleu, where Raimund became deputy head of the Escola Nova Unificada. They left for France in 1937 and stayed with Louise’s family. Gutrune remembered visiting them there when the twins were two. [Torelló history][torello] · [Gutrune’s chronicle][chronicle]

### Teaching and family life in Venezuela

Raimund went on to Venezuela, where he taught at the Liceo Libertador and the Universidad de los Andes in Mérida. His family joined him there. Molera i Clota reports that in 1941 Raimund founded the Minerva publishing house to produce workbooks for his students. He retired from university teaching in 1970. [Torelló history][torello]

Gutrune visited Raimund and Louise in January 1973 and remembered bougainvillea filling the garden around their house and studio. In a 1987 letter to Margrit, Raimund wrote about the cool mountain mornings in Mérida and mentioned that he was still working at his desk on a chapter about ethics. He died in Mérida on **7 July 1990**. [Gutrune’s chronicle][chronicle] · [Raimund’s 1987 letter](../../records/letters/L005.md) · [Rostock bookplate catalogue][rostock]
