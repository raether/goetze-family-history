# Raimund Götze

*1899–1990*

[Biography index](../README.md)

## Biography

Raimund was the brother closest to Gutrune in age. She records that he was born on 8 October 1899 in Naunhof near Leipzig. She recalls their childhood in Heppenheim, including climbing the Starkenburg ruins and using a walnut tree as a place to do their schoolwork.

Raimund studied physics. Gutrune remembers him working as an assistant in Frankfurt. A history by Santi Molera i Clota records that he presented his doctoral thesis at Tübingen in 1921 and later worked at the Einstein Tower in Potsdam. It also describes his years in Torelló, Catalonia, where he taught at the Aurora workers’ school, ran a photography studio, and helped organize Catalan grammar courses in 1932 and 1935. [Santi Molera i Clota’s Torelló history (S10)](https://elcosidordigital.blogspot.com/2012/10/raimund-gotze.html)

Molera i Clota dates Raimund’s marriage to Marie Louise Lagrange at the Torelló justice of the peace court to **16 September 1934** and the birth of their twins to **14 April 1935**. Gutrune gives the same wedding day and month but types **1943**. The 1934 date fits her account of the family leaving Spain with the twins during the Civil War. The original marriage entry would confirm the date. [Santi Molera i Clota’s Torelló history (S10)](https://elcosidordigital.blogspot.com/2012/10/raimund-gotze.html) · [Gutrune’s chronicle](../../records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#dr-raimund-goetze)

After leaving Spain for France, Raimund went on to Venezuela. Molera i Clota describes his teaching at the Liceo Libertador and the Universidad de los Andes in Mérida. He dates Raimund’s university retirement to 1970 and his death in Mérida to **7 July 1990**. Gutrune recalls visiting Raimund and Louise there in 1973. [Santi Molera i Clota’s Torelló history (S10)](https://elcosidordigital.blogspot.com/2012/10/raimund-gotze.html) · [Gutrune’s chronicle](../../records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#dr-raimund-goetze)
