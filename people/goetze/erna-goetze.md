# Erna Götze (married Werner Holz)

*Born 1896 in the chronicle*

[Biography index](../README.md)

## Biography

Erna was a daughter of [Bernhard Götze](bernhard-goetze.md) and [Johanna Römmler](../roemmler/johanna-roemmler.md). Gutrune records her birth in Glauchau.

Erna married the merchant Werner Holz, the eldest son of her paternal aunt Frieda. Gutrune says they divorced in 1936. She remembers Erna living in Berlin near her cousin Hermann’s daughter Irmgard in later life.

Like Hildegard, Erna was sent to Heppenheim for a year to learn to cook with her aunt Else. Gutrune remembers the sisters joking about their aunt’s generous cooking. In later years Erna became friends with her cousin Hermann’s daughter Irmgard, according to the chronicle.
