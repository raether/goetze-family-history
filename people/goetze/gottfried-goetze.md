# Gottfried Götze

*1898–1965 in the chronicle*

[Biography index](../README.md)

## Biography

Gottfried, Gutrune’s brother, was born on 4 April 1898 in Naunhof near Leipzig, according to the chronicle. Gutrune remembers their Sunday walks in the Bombach valley and the fairy tales he invented about woodland animals, elves, and dwarfs.

Gottfried was called up at seventeen during the First World War and laid electrical cables on the Western Front. He and Gutrune exchanged letters while he served. After the war he joined the Bergfried settlement for a time, trained in agriculture, worked on a farm in Upper Hesse, and completed a doctorate in natural science at Giessen, according to Gutrune. She says he met Margret Ulbrich at the school community in Oberhambach, where Margret taught younger children. [Gutrune’s chronicle, Gottfried](../../records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#dr-gottfried-goetze)

His agricultural work came to focus on bees. The University of Hohenheim’s biographical dictionary dates his directorship of the Beekeeping Teaching and Research Institute in **Mayen near Koblenz** to 1932–1951. That agrees with the location given on page 27 of the chronicle. He lectured in Bonn from 1941 and became an associate professor there in 1951. His research covered bee forage, honey quality, and ways of distinguishing European bee breeds. His books included *Die beste Biene* (1940) and *Leitfaden der Bienenzucht* (1950). [Hohenheim Dictionary of Agricultural Biographies, p. 341](https://uniarchiv.uni-hohenheim.de/fileadmin/einrichtungen/uniarchiv/Agrarbiografien/Hohenheim_Dictionary_of_Agricultural_Biographies_2025.pdf) · [Gutrune’s chronicle, Gottfried](../../records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#dr-gottfried-goetze)

Gottfried married Margret Ulbrich on 23 March 1923 in Berlin-Lichtenberg, according to Gutrune. They had two sons and five daughters. Gutrune records that Gottfried died in 1965, aged sixty-seven. The Hohenheim dictionary gives **4 April 1965** in Bad Godesberg. [Hohenheim Dictionary of Agricultural Biographies, p. 341](https://uniarchiv.uni-hohenheim.de/fileadmin/einrichtungen/uniarchiv/Agrarbiografien/Hohenheim_Dictionary_of_Agricultural_Biographies_2025.pdf)
