# Gottfried Götze

*1898–1965 in the chronicle*

[Biography index](../README.md)

## Biography

Gottfried, Gutrune’s brother, was born on 4 April 1898 in Naunhof near Leipzig, according to the chronicle. Gutrune remembers their Sunday walks in the Bombach valley and the fairy tales he invented about woodland animals, elves, and dwarfs.

During the First World War he served on the Western Front laying cables. He studied agriculture and natural science after the war, earned a doctorate, directed a beekeeping school, and later taught in Bonn. The chronicle places the beekeeping school in Mayen in the Rhineland on page 27 and uses a different regional description on page 28.

Gottfried married Margret Ulbrich on 23 March 1923 in Berlin-Lichtenberg, according to Gutrune. They had two sons and five daughters. Gutrune records that Gottfried died in 1965, aged sixty-seven.
