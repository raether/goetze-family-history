# Gutrune Falckon, née Götze

*1903–1989*

[Biography index](../README.md)

## Biography

Gutrune was born on 8 June 1903 in Naunhof near Leipzig, as recorded in her signed 1938 declaration of intention. In her chronicle she identifies [Rudolf Götze](rudolf-goetze.md) and [Else Römmler](../roemmler/else-roemmler.md) as her parents and names [Gunter](gunter-goetze.md) as her twin brother.

After training as a gardener, she worked at a nursery in Zwingenberg run by Lene Knauer, who became a close friend. She later moved to Berlin, lived with her sister Gertraute, and retrained in therapeutic exercise when gardening no longer supported her. [Gutrune’s chronicle, Gutrune](../../records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#gutrune)

The passenger manifest records her arrival in New York on 13 July 1938. She married Theodore Falcke in Manhattan in 1942. The archive’s marriage summary gives 7 November and her chronicle says 11 November. The chronicle also uses the name Theodore Falckon.

Gutrune describes working as a masseuse in New York while she lacked a U.S. physical therapy license. She and Theo moved to Los Angeles in 1944 and later to New Zealand. In Dunedin she studied and volunteered in a university hospital before passing a local examination. In Honolulu she volunteered at Tripler Army Hospital during a polio outbreak, qualified under the territorial rules, and became director of physical therapy at St. Francis Hospital. She later took a position at a veterans’ hospital in Oakland. [Gutrune’s chronicle, Gutrune](../../records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#gutrune)

In her account she spent twelve years at the Oakland hospital and retired in 1970. She cared for Theo before his death in 1971 and later took Gertraute into her Berkeley home. There she wrote the family chronicle in 1988 from her own memories, relatives’ stories, and the family tree. A California death-index transcription records her death on **14 December 1989**.
