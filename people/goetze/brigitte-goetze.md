# Brigitte Spieler, née Götze

*1901–1977 in the chronicle*

[Biography index](../README.md)

## Biography

Brigitte was the youngest of the three daughters of [Bernhard Götze](bernhard-goetze.md) and [Johanna Römmler](../roemmler/johanna-roemmler.md). Gutrune records her birth in Glauchau and recalls their childhood visits and Brigitte’s love of horses.

Gutrune says Brigitte married the veterinarian Hubert Spieler in 1926 and that they lived through the Second World War in Saxony. The chronicle gives 1977 as the year of her death. Its typed line and a handwritten annotation give different places, which remain to be checked against another record.

Gutrune remembers visiting Brigitte in Berlin, where her uncle gave the girls arithmetic questions and Brigitte made her father laugh by putting her own words into the family’s grace before meals. Brigitte spent the years of wartime food shortages in Berlin with her parents. After the war, when the family lived in Grünheide, her father gave her a riding horse. Gutrune says Grünheide was also where Brigitte married Hubert.
