# Gunter Götze

*Died in infancy in 1903 according to the chronicle*

[Biography index](../README.md)

## Biography

Gutrune identifies Gunter as her twin brother and the seventh child of [Rudolf Götze](rudolf-goetze.md) and [Else Römmler](../roemmler/else-roemmler.md). She says that he died after only a few days. [Gutrune’s](gutrune-goetze.md) own birth is documented as 8 June 1903 in Naunhof. Gunter’s birth and death entries remain to be located.
