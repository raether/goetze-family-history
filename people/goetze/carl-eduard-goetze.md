# Carl Eduard Götze

*Textile manufacturer in Glauchau, documented in 1856*

[Biography index](../README.md)

## Biography

The 1856 *Album der Sächsischen Industrie* names Carl Eduard Götze and [Emil Hermann Götze](emil-hermann-goetze.md) as the owners of *Götze senior und Söhne*. The firm sold wool and blended fabrics at home and abroad. Its Glauchau premises handled finishing, and weavers in the town and surrounding area worked outside the factory. Emil Hermann’s biography gives a fuller account of how the business operated. [1856 industry profile, pp. 149–150 (S1)](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/G%C3%B6tze_senior_und_S%C3%B6hne)

## Family

The profile establishes a business partnership with Emil Hermann. A family or civil record is still needed to determine whether they were related and to establish Carl Eduard’s life dates.
