# Albrecht Götze

*1897–1971 in the chronicle*

[Biography index](../README.md)

## Biography

Gutrune gives Albrecht’s birth as 11 January 1897 in Leipzig. He studied ancient languages and was wounded in the leg while serving on the Western Front during the First World War. After the war he pursued Assyriology and held academic posts in Germany.

According to Gutrune, Albrecht was a Social Democrat and spoke out against the persecution of Jews. He left Nazi Germany and obtained a position at Yale University. His research took him to archaeological sites abroad. Gutrune remembers his stories of traveling and riding with Arab hosts and his later visit to Raimund in Venezuela.

The chronicle names Dieter, Marianne, and Alle as his children and records his death on 15 August 1971 in Garmisch-Partenkirchen. Dieter later shared family research with Gutrune and received a copy of the chronicle.
