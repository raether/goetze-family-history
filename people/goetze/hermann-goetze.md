# Hermann Götze

*1893–1962 in the chronicle*

[Biography index](../README.md)

## Biography

Gutrune’s eldest brother Hermann was born in Würzburg on 3 April 1893, according to the chronicle. He had a gift for languages and trained for a career in foreign affairs. He went to Rome to learn Italian and later to Paris, then studied Turkish and Arabic in Berlin along with law. As a schoolboy in Bensheim he brought friends to the family home in Heppenheim, where they held musical evenings. One of those friendships was with Erich van Hoboken-Schmidt, whose sister Gertrud he later married. [Gutrune’s chronicle, Hermann](../../records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#dr-hermann-goetze)

During the First World War he served in the artillery on the Eastern Front. Gutrune recalls a letter about spending Christmas 1914 in a snowstorm in Poland and later being hospitalized. As a lieutenant he was transferred to Turkey and served as an interpreter in the Dardanelles. [Gutrune’s chronicle, Hermann](../../records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#dr-hermann-goetze)

He married Gertrud van Hoboken-Schmidt while on leave. Their daughter Irmgard was born in Alsbach in 1918. After the war he commuted from Heppenheim to Heidelberg to finish his law doctorate. In Berlin, his uncle Bernhard trained him to sell textiles. He later built an auditing business whose clients included major book publishers. In his 1937 letter to Gottfried he enclosed a trade article on plant breeding. [Gutrune’s chronicle, Hermann](../../records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#dr-hermann-goetze) · [Hermann’s 1937 letter](../../records/letters/L002.md) Gutrune recalls that he enjoyed astrology in later life and records his death in Berlin on 12 May 1962.

The 1938 manifest names Gutrune’s brother in Berlin as H. Goetze. The initial and location support the identification with Hermann.
