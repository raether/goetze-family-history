# Albertine Friedericke Caroline Brunner

*Born 1830, died about 1912 according to the chronicle*

Gutrune remembered her paternal grandmother as Friederike Brunner, a devout woman who dressed in black and gave her grandchildren chocolate. The account of the 1857 Frankfurt marriage cited in the archive gives her name as Albertine Friedericke Caroline Brunner. The Dresden register summary reads Albertine Caroline, née Brunner, with the full sequence of given names still uncertain.

She was a daughter of Frankfurt pharmacist Lorenz Gottlieb Brunner and Anna Maria Enders. She married [Emil Hermann Götze](emil-hermann-goetze.md) on 18 November 1857, according to Gutrune. Among their surviving children were [Rudolf](rudolf-goetze.md) and [Bernhard](bernhard-goetze.md).

Gutrune recalled that Friederike wrote an account of her youth in Frankfurt and left a large family Bible to Gertraute, who later passed it to Dieter. Friederike’s precise date of death remains to be confirmed.
