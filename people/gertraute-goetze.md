# Gertraute Götze Fischer

*1895–1981 in the chronicle*

Gertraute was Gutrune’s older sister. Gutrune remembers her writing poetry as a girl and going to the Leipzig Opera with their father. As an adult she worked in foreign-language business correspondence and later in the library of Berlin’s Altes Museum. Her son Franzl died of diphtheria in 1934 at the age of seven.

Gertraute left Germany and worked in New York, where she met and married Walter Fischer. They later settled in California. Gutrune cared for Gertraute in her final years and records that she died of pneumonia on 18 November 1981.

Gutrune identifies Gertraute by name as her sister. The 1938 manifest separately names a sister G. Fischer in New York, and the exile archive has a 1937 application under Gertraute’s name. A birth or marriage record remains a priority for confirming her parentage.
