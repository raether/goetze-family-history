# Auguste Arndt, née Heinigke

*1824–1874 in the chronicle*

[Biography index](../README.md)

## Biography

Gutrune records Auguste Heinigke’s birth in Gablenz near Chemnitz on 29 July 1824 and her death in Chemnitz on 4 December 1874. She married the painter [Gottfried Ludwig Arndt](../arndt/gottfried-ludwig-arndt.md), and their elder daughter [Helene](../arndt/helene-arndt.md) became Gutrune’s maternal grandmother.

Gutrune’s mother remembered visiting Auguste as a child and watching her eat cherries on the balcony. The chronicle says a younger daughter later went to America, but leaves her unnamed. The stated ages at their marriage, seventeen for Auguste and thirty-one for Gottfried Ludwig, point to about 1841, although the manuscript types 1941. The birth and death dates imply an age of fifty at death, while the memoir says she was in her forty-ninth year. Church or civil entries could help settle these dates and identify the younger daughter.
