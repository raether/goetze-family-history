# Gunter Götze

*Died in infancy in 1903 according to the chronicle*

Gutrune identifies Gunter as her twin brother and the seventh child of [Rudolf Götze](rudolf-goetze.md) and [Else Römmler](else-roemmler.md). She says that he died after only a few days. [Gutrune’s](gutrune-goetze.md) own birth is documented as 8 June 1903 in Naunhof. Gunter’s birth and death entries remain to be located.

## Sources

- [Gutrune Falckon’s 1988 family chronicle](../records/chronicles/gutrune-1988.md), German manuscript pp. 17, 31.
