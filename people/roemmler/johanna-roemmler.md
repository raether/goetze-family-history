# Johanna Götze, née Römmler

*Born 1872 in the chronicle*

[Biography index](../README.md)

## Biography

Johanna Römmler was one of the daughters of [Emil Römmler](emil-roemmler.md) and [Helene Arndt](../arndt/helene-arndt.md). Gutrune calls her Hanne or Hannchen and identifies her as the youngest sister of [Else](else-roemmler.md).

Johanna married [Bernhard Götze](../goetze/bernhard-goetze.md), the brother of Else’s husband Rudolf. Gutrune lists Johanna and Bernhard’s three daughters: [Hildegard](../goetze/hildegard-goetze.md), [Erna](../goetze/erna-goetze.md), and [Brigitte](../goetze/brigitte-goetze.md). The memoir remembers visits between the two households and says that Hildegard and Erna each spent a year with Else learning to cook.
