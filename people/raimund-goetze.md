# Raimund Götze

*Born 1899*

[Biography index](README.md)

## Biography

Raimund was the brother closest to Gutrune in age. She records that he was born on 8 October 1899 in Naunhof near Leipzig. She recalls their childhood in Heppenheim, including climbing the Starkenburg ruins and using a walnut tree as a place to do their schoolwork.

Raimund studied physics and became an assistant in Frankfurt. His career later took him to Barcelona and to the Universidad de los Andes in Mérida, Venezuela. He married Louise Lagrange. Gutrune describes the family’s flight to France during the Spanish Civil War and her visit to their home in Mérida in 1973.

The typed marriage year is 1943. Gutrune’s narrative places the marriage and the birth of their twins before the family’s departure from Spain. The date needs a marriage record.
