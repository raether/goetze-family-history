# Hildegard Götze

*Born 1894 in the chronicle*

Hildegard, called Hilde, was a daughter of [Bernhard Götze](bernhard-goetze.md) and [Johanna Römmler](johanna-roemmler.md). Gutrune gives her birthplace as Glauchau and recalls that Hilde spent a year with Gutrune’s mother in Heppenheim to learn to cook.

The chronicle says Hildegard married Frank Glatzel in Berlin on 27 November 1918 and later divorced. Gutrune records that Hildegard was living in Berlin when she wrote her account.
