# Family biographies

These profiles follow people described in [Gutrune Falckon’s 1988 family chronicle](../records/chronicles/gutrune-1988.md). The chronicle and the surviving [family charts](../charts/README.md) provide additional context. Each profile cites the manuscript pages and identifies civil or archival records where used. Each person is listed once, under their birth family. The Family section of each profile links to relatives across family lines.
