# Carl Emil Römmler

*1842–1941 in the chronicle*

[Biography index](README.md)

## Biography

Carl Emil Römmler was a Dresden photographer and fine-art printer. The Dresden register identifies him as the photographer father of [Else Römmler](else-roemmler.md) and records his signature as a witness at her marriage. Gutrune gives his birthplace as Mitweida and styles his name Karl Emil Roemmler.

According to the chronicle, he left home with three thalers and a camera. Gutrune remembered reading his memoir in Raimund’s studio in Mérida. In her retelling, he photographed children he met near the Dresden palace who turned out to be the king’s children. She says he subsequently became a court photographer and received a commission to photograph works in Dresden’s art galleries.

He married [Helene Arndt](helene-arndt.md) in Chemnitz in 1862. Gutrune describes a trip to Paris to learn three-color photography and names the printing business as Roemmler and Jonas before he continued on his own. She remembers the company’s floral and gallery postcards and traces its later decline through the inflation years.
