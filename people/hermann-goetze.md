# Hermann Götze

*1893–1962 in the chronicle*

Gutrune’s eldest brother Hermann was born in Würzburg on 3 April 1893, according to the chronicle. He had a gift for languages and trained for a career in foreign affairs. During the First World War he served first in the artillery and later as a Turkish interpreter in the Dardanelles.

He married Gertrud van Hoboken-Schmidt while on leave. Their daughter Irmgard was born in Alsbach in 1918. After the war Hermann completed a doctorate in law, moved to Berlin, and worked in textile sales before building an auditing business. Gutrune recalls that he enjoyed astrology in later life and records his death in Berlin on 12 May 1962.

The 1938 manifest names Gutrune’s brother in Berlin as H. Goetze. The initial and location support the identification with Hermann.
