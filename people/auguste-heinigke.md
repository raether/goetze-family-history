# Auguste Arndt, née Heinigke

*1824–1874 in the chronicle*

Gutrune records Auguste Heinigke’s birth in Gablenz near Chemnitz on 29 July 1824 and her death in Chemnitz on 4 December 1874. She married the painter [Gottfried Ludwig Arndt](gottfried-ludwig-arndt.md), and their elder daughter [Helene](helene-arndt.md) became Gutrune’s maternal grandmother.

Gutrune’s mother remembered visiting Auguste as a child and watching her eat cherries on the balcony. The chronicle says a younger daughter later went to America, but leaves her unnamed. Auguste’s own birth and death dates, the marriage year, and the younger daughter’s identity are leads for church or civil registers.
