# Questions where relatives can help

These are **specific gaps**, not invitations to fill blanks with guesses.

1. **Glauchau, 1863:** Rudolf's parents are now established by the **1892 Dresden civil marriage record**, but can we find his original **1863 baptism entry** for the exact birthplace address and godparents? [New evidence](records/marriages/1892-dresden-goetze-roemmler.md).
2. **Glauchau business, 1856–1884:** Do trade records, company papers, or family letters describe the later history of *Götze senior und Söhne*, its property or its sales?
3. **The children of Rudolf and Elsa:** Can dated civil records establish a complete list of children and their birthplaces? Published sources disagree on the number.
4. **Berlin Wagner association, 1887–1888:** Is Rudolf recorded as a *formal member* or merely a speaker? The proposed source is the Humboldt University Archive, *Rektor und Senat*, No. 683. The publicly available 1888 society report did not reveal its member list.
5. **Alzey, 1920:** Can a death certificate, contemporary obituary, or local newspaper account clarify the circumstances of Rudolf's death? The precise cause has not been verified.
6. **California, 1989:** Can an official death certificate and earlier birth or marriage documents establish Gutrune Falckon's full given names, parents, and migration history? Avoid including social-security identifiers.
7. **Gertraute Goetze-Fischer:** Can any primary birth or marriage record establish whether she belongs to Rudolf and Elsa's family? She should not be merged with Gutrune on the strength of similar names.
8. **Transatlantic family papers:** Do any US relatives hold dated letters, photographs with captions, obituaries, immigration records, or correspondence from Germany? We would welcome references and descriptions before any upload.

[Submit a research lead](https://github.com/Contexter/goetze-family-history/issues/new/choose) or follow [the contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md).

**Stop rule:** A catalogue listing is not a viewed manuscript; a matching surname is not a proven kinship; a family memory is a valuable memory, not a verified record.
