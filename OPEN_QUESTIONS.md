# Open family history questions

If you have a record, a family copy, or a clue about where to look, a name, date, place, and source reference can help move one of these questions forward.

## Family relationships and marriage dates

- **Rudolf Götze’s baptism, Glauchau, 1863.** The 1892 Dresden marriage register names his parents and records his birth date. Can the original baptism entry give the family’s address and name his godparents? [Dresden marriage register](records/marriages/1892-dresden-goetze-roemmler.md)
- **The mothers named in the 1892 Dresden register.** Can a clearer copy establish the full given names of Rudolf’s mother, Albertine Brunner, and settle the reading of Else’s mother’s name? The register summary provisionally reads *Johanne*, while Gutrune identifies her maternal grandmother as *Helene Arndt*. Can Else’s birth or baptism entry help reconcile the readings? [Dresden marriage register](records/marriages/1892-dresden-goetze-roemmler.md) · [Else’s biography](people/roemmler/else-roemmler.md)
- **Bernhard Götze and Johanna Römmler.** Where and when did the couple marry? Their marriage was the second between the Götze brothers and the Römmler sisters. A civil or church entry could also help confirm their parents’ names. [Johanna’s biography](people/roemmler/johanna-roemmler.md)
- **The children of Rudolf Götze and Else Römmler.** Can birth and baptism records confirm Gutrune’s list of seven children and their birthplaces? In particular, can we find birth and death entries for Gutrune’s twin, Gunter, who died in infancy according to the chronicle? [Chronicle, Rudolf and Else’s children](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#the-children-of-rudolf-goetze-and-else-roemmler) · [Gunter’s biography](people/goetze/gunter-goetze.md)
- **Gertraute Götze Fischer’s parentage.** Is there a birth or marriage record naming her parents? Gutrune identifies Gertraute as her sister, and the 1938 arrival manifest separately names a sister G. Fischer in New York. [Gertraute’s biography](people/goetze/gertraute-goetze.md) · [1938 passenger manifest](records/Getrune%20Goetze/1938%20Passinger%20Manifest.md)
- **Gottfried Ludwig Arndt and Auguste Heinigke, Chemnitz.** Can a church marriage entry confirm the year? The chronicle types **1941**, while the stated ages suggest **1841**. [Chronicle, Arndt and Heinigke families](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#the-arndt-and-heinigke-families)
- **Maria Susanna and Christoph Pfeiler, Elbing.** Can a marriage or burial entry establish Maria Susanna’s surname and the couple’s marriage date? The chronicle gives *De Noël* or *von Neaus*, with a handwritten note questioning the initial, and the marriage year was corrected on the manuscript. Gutrune’s suggestion that Maria Susanna was a French Protestant is a lead to investigate. [Chronicle, Arndt and Heinigke families](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#the-arndt-and-heinigke-families)
- **Raimund Götze and Louise Lagrange, Torelló.** Can we find the original marriage entry at the Torelló justice of the peace court? Molera i Clota gives **16 September 1934**, which fits Gutrune’s account of the family leaving Spain with their twins during the Civil War. Her chronicle types **16 September 1943**. [Santi Molera i Clota’s Torelló history (S10)](https://elcosidordigital.blogspot.com/2012/10/raimund-gotze.html) · [Chronicle, Raimund](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#dr-raimund-goetze)
- **Gutrune Götze and Theodore Falcke, Manhattan, 1942.** What dates does the original record give for the license and ceremony, and how does it spell Gutrune’s mother’s name? The index gives **7 November** and reads the mother’s name as *Lesa*, while Gutrune’s chronicle gives **11 November** for the marriage. [Marriage record summary and original scan](records/Getrune%20Goetze/Gutrune_Goetze_Marriage_License_1942.md) · [Chronicle, Gutrune](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#gutrune)

## Work, migration, and later records

- **Götze senior und Söhne in Glauchau and a possible New York branch.** Can trade directories or company papers trace the Glauchau firm after its 1856 published profile? Gutrune also passes on a recollection of a possible New York branch. Can directories, advertisements, or company records confirm whether it existed, when it opened, and who ran it? [Historical source S1](SOURCES.md) · [Chronicle research leads](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#leads-for-family-research)
- **Rudolf’s role in the Berlin Wagner association, 1887–1888.** Does the Humboldt University Archive’s *Rektor und Senat* file No. 683 name him as a formal member? A published lecture is attributed to him. [Historical source S5](SOURCES.md)
- **Rudolf’s death in Alzey, 1920.** Can a civil death entry, obituary, or contemporary account confirm the circumstances of his death? Gutrune describes his transfer to Alzey in both her letter to Lore and her later chronicle. [1983 letter to Lore](records/letters/L004.md) · [Chronicle, Rudolf](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#my-father-dr-rudolf-emil-goetze)
- **Gutrune’s whereabouts in December 1940.** Can the letter’s original page, envelope, or postmark clarify where it was written? The transcription heading gives Mayen, while the letter says she was again living on Walton Avenue and mentions a place tentatively read as Trier. [1940 letter to Margret and Gottfried](records/letters/L003.md)
- **Gutrune’s death and names, California, 1989.** Can the official death certificate or an obituary confirm her full recorded name and death details? Her 1938 passenger manifest and declaration already establish her arrival in New York and record her stated birth date and birthplace. [Immigration records](records/Getrune%20Goetze/README.md) · [California death-index transcription, S11](SOURCES.md)

## Family papers and letters

- **Brunner portraits, family Bible, and memoirs.** Do relatives have the Brunner and Enders portraits, Friedericke Brunner’s account of her youth, her family Bible, or Emil Römmler’s memoir? Gutrune suggests the portraits and Brunner account may have been with Alle Lee, says the Bible went from Gertraute to Dieter, and recalls reading Emil’s memoir in Raimund’s studio in Mérida. [Chronicle research leads](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#leads-for-family-research)
- **German transcriptions for two early letters.** Can someone read the original German in Albrecht’s 1935 letter to Margret and Hermann’s 1937 letter to Gottfried, including the enclosure where legible? Both have English translations and original scans. [Albrecht’s letter](records/letters/L001.md) · [Hermann’s letter and enclosure](records/letters/L002.md)
- **Other family papers.** Do relatives in the United States or elsewhere have dated letters, captioned photographs, obituaries, or immigration records that could help answer these questions? A description and source reference are enough to start. [Contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md)
