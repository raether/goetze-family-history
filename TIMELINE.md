# Family timeline

Follow the Götze, Römmler, Arndt, Brunner, and Heinigke families through dated events in the archive. The source column distinguishes civil and immigration records, published accounts, indexes, and Gutrune Falckon’s family recollections. Dates described as approximate or disputed remain open for research.

## 1800–1892 · Glauchau, Frankfurt, Chemnitz, and Dresden

| Date | Event | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |

| 4 December 1874 | [Auguste Heinigke](people/heinigke/auguste-heinigke.md) died in Chemnitz, according to Gutrune. | [Chronicle, Arndt and Heinigke families](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#the-arndt-and-heinigke-families) |

| 8 April 1892 | [Rudolf Götze](people/goetze/rudolf-goetze.md) and [Else Römmler](people/roemmler/else-roemmler.md) married in Dresden. Gutrune also records a second marriage between the same two families. Rudolf’s brother [Bernhard Götze](people/goetze/bernhard-goetze.md) married Else’s sister [Johanna Römmler](people/roemmler/johanna-roemmler.md). The archive still needs a date for their marriage. [Chronicle, Römmler family](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#the-roemmler-family)

An earlier timeline also noted a possible 1849 Frankfurt marriage connection to the Wurster family. The cited source-register entry documents the 1857 Brunner connection. The 1849 entry still needs a specific citation. [Source register, S3](SOURCES.md)

## 1893–1920 · Two households and the move to Hesse

| Date | Event | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1893–1894 | Rudolf took part in an organized antisemitic campaign against the Jewish Schmul family’s legally approved use of the surname Goetze. Contemporary newspapers reproduced his August 1893 letter and reported on the campaign. | [Source register, S6–S7](SOURCES.md) |
| 1893–1899 | Gutrune dates the births of five of Rudolf and Else’s children to these years. [Hermann](people/goetze/hermann-goetze.md) was born in Würzburg in 1893, [Gertraute](people/goetze/gertraute-goetze.md) in 1895, [Albrecht](people/goetze/albrecht-goetze.md) in Leipzig in 1897, and [Gottfried](people/goetze/gottfried-goetze.md) and [Raimund](people/goetze/raimund-goetze.md) in Naunhof in 1898 and 1899. | [Chronicle, manuscript pp. 17–30](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988.md#page-guide) |
| 1894–1901 | Gutrune dates the births of Bernhard and Johanna’s three daughters in Glauchau to [Hildegard](people/goetze/hildegard-goetze.md) in 1894, [Erna](people/goetze/erna-goetze.md) in 1896, and [Brigitte](people/goetze/brigitte-goetze.md) in 1901. | [Chronicle, Hildegard, Erna, and Brigitte](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#hildegard-erna-and-brigitte-goetze) |
| 1902 | [Gottfried Ludwig Arndt](people/arndt/gottfried-ludwig-arndt.md) died in Dresden on 4 February. Gutrune also records [Emil Hermann Götze’s](people/goetze/emil-hermann-goetze.md) death in 1902. | [Chronicle, manuscript pp. 1–5 and 13–17](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988.md#page-guide) |
| 8 June 1903 | [Gutrune Götze](people/goetze/gutrune-goetze.md) was born in Naunhof, as she recorded in her signed 1938 declaration. Her chronicle names [Gunter](people/goetze/gunter-goetze.md) as her twin brother and says he died after a few days. His birth and death entries are still sought. | [Declaration of intention](records/Getrune%20Goetze/README.md#1938-declaration-of-intention) · [Chronicle, Rudolf and Else’s children](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#the-children-of-rudolf-goetze-and-else-roemmler) |
| Around 1908 | After a failed water-tower venture in Naunhof, [Rudolf](people/goetze/rudolf-goetze.md) took a post at the state institution in Heppenheim. [Else](people/roemmler/else-roemmler.md) first moved with the children to Darmstadt, and the household later settled in Heppenheim. | [Chronicle, Rudolf](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#my-father-dr-rudolf-emil-goetze) |
| 1914–1918 | During the First World War, [Hermann](people/goetze/hermann-goetze.md) served in the artillery and later as a Turkish interpreter. [Albrecht](people/goetze/albrecht-goetze.md) and [Gottfried](people/goetze/gottfried-goetze.md) served on the Western Front, where Albrecht was wounded. | [Chronicle, manuscript pp. 19–28](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988.md#page-guide) |
| 30 October 1920 | [Rudolf](people/goetze/rudolf-goetze.md) died in Alzey. The archive still seeks an official record for the circumstances of his death. | [Chronicle, Rudolf](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#my-father-dr-rudolf-emil-goetze) · [Source register, S4](SOURCES.md) |

## 1923–1945 · Berlin, emigration, and wartime moves

| Date | Event | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 23 March 1923 | [Gottfried Götze](people/goetze/gottfried-goetze.md) married Margret Ulbrich in Berlin-Lichtenberg, according to Gutrune. He later directed a beekeeping school and taught in Bonn. | [Chronicle, Gottfried](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#dr-gottfried-goetze) |
| 1927–1935 | [Gertraute’s](people/goetze/gertraute-goetze.md) son Franzl was born in 1927 and died in 1934. [Else](people/roemmler/else-roemmler.md), who had helped care for him in Berlin, died in April 1935. | [Chronicle, Gutrune](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#gutrune) |
| By 1935 | [Albrecht](people/goetze/albrecht-goetze.md) had left Nazi Germany and taken a position in Assyriology at Yale University, according to Gutrune. | [Chronicle, Gertraute and Albrecht](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#gertraute-18951981) |
| During the Spanish Civil War | Gutrune recalls [Raimund](people/goetze/raimund-goetze.md) and Louise Lagrange leaving Spain with their twins. A Torelló local history dates their marriage to **16 September 1934**. The chronicle types **1943**. The original marriage entry is still sought. | [Chronicle, Raimund](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#dr-raimund-goetze) · [Santi Molera i Clota’s Torelló history (S10)](https://elcosidordigital.blogspot.com/2012/10/raimund-gotze.html) |
| 1937 | An exile archive preserves [Gertraute Götze Fischer’s](people/goetze/gertraute-goetze.md) application. Gutrune identifies Gertraute as her sister. A civil birth or marriage record confirming Gertraute’s parentage remains a research goal. | [Source register, S8](SOURCES.md) · [Chronicle, Gertraute](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#gertraute-18951981) |
| 13 July and 14 October 1938 | [Gutrune](people/goetze/gutrune-goetze.md) arrived in New York aboard the *Statendam* on 13 July and signed a declaration of intention on 14 October. The arrival manifest names a sister G. Fischer in New York and a brother H. Goetze in Berlin. | [Passenger manifest and declaration](records/Getrune%20Goetze/README.md) |
| 1938–1939 | Nazi authorities pressured the Jewish Goetze family involved in the earlier naming dispute to resume the surname Schmul. The archive treats this family as a separate subject of historical research. | [Source register, S9](SOURCES.md) |
| 1941 | [Carl Emil Römmler](people/roemmler/emil-roemmler.md) died in Dresden, according to Gutrune. | [Chronicle, Römmler family](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#the-roemmler-family) |
| November 1942 | [Gutrune](people/goetze/gutrune-goetze.md) married Theodore Falcke in Manhattan. The marriage index gives **7 November**. The chronicle gives **11 November** and also uses the surname Falckon. | [Manhattan marriage license summary, No. 22738](records/Getrune%20Goetze/Gutrune_Goetze_Marriage_License_1942.md) · [Chronicle, Gutrune](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#gutrune) |
| 1944 | Gutrune and Theodore moved from New York to Los Angeles, according to her account. | [Chronicle, Gutrune](records/chronicles/gutrune-1988-english.md#gutrune) |
