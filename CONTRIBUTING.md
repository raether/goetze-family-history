# Contribute to our shared family research

**Welcome, relatives in the US and Germany.** You need no genealogy software, programming experience, or scholarly vocabulary. A family letter, photograph, chart, memory, or uncertain clue is a valuable contribution in its own right.

You do not need to prove a historical claim or assign an evidence category before contributing. The archive's editors take responsibility for indexing, checking sources, preserving uncertainty, and distinguishing records from interpretations. For privately held documents we can work from a description first and agree on what may be published.

## The simplest way to help

Read [OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md). If you have a clue, [open a new GitHub issue](https://github.com/Contexter/goetze-family-history/issues/new/choose). An issue can be as simple as: "I found a dated 1942 wedding notice with the name X; here is the newspaper and page."

If you prefer the existing **family Facebook group**, you can discuss a lead there. Ask the family researcher before transferring anyone else's Facebook post into this public archive. **Nothing from Facebook is imported automatically.** If you want to propose an edited research note, you can also open a pull request.

## Helpful context (not an entrance requirement)

- Which deceased historical person or family branch does it concern?
- What is the exact **date and place**?
- Is it an **original document**, a **published biography**, a **family recollection**, or an **interpretation**?
- What is its source? Give a public URL, archive call number, book page, or photo caption if possible.
- What is still uncertain or contradictory?
- If you own a photo or letter, do you **consent to its public display**, and do you hold the necessary rights? If not, describe it without uploading it.

## Privacy and editorial care

**Do not publicly post** details identifying living relatives without their informed permission: addresses, full birth dates, contact information, financial information, medical history, IDs, social-security numbers, or private correspondence. Avoid identifying living minors entirely.

For family documents held privately, start with a **description**, not a public scan. No one should feel obliged to share family material. Be careful with uncertain paternity and relationships; a same-name match alone does not establish lineage.

We will publish painful historical facts—including documented antisemitism and Nazi persecution—with clear citations and without assuming guilt or beliefs for descendants. We welcome corrections without judgment.

## Research vocabulary used by the editors

**DIRECT:** An original historical document actually viewed and cited.

**SECONDARY:** A reliable scholarly or biographical account.

**FAMILY MEMORY:** Attributed oral recollection, not independent proof.

**HYPOTHESIS:** A proposed connection awaiting a named record.

**CONFLICT:** Two or more sources that disagree, kept visible until resolved.

If in doubt, mark the statement **unverified**. An honest gap is better than a polished but unsupported story.
