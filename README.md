# The Götze / Goetze Family History Archive

This archive brings together the stories and records of the Götze, Römmler, Arndt, Brunner, and Heinigke families and related branches. It is a shared place for relatives and researchers to explore family connections, read original material and translations, and contribute new findings.

[Deutsche Einführung](DEUTSCH.md)

## Browse the archive

| Start with | What you’ll find |
| --- | --- |

| [Historical sources](SOURCES.md) · [Open research questions](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) | What the sources tell us and where more evidence would help. |

The archive cites family recollections alongside civil records, letters, and published research. When sources differ on a name, date, or relationship, the relevant page records the different readings.

## Contribute

A caption on the back of a photograph, a family story, or a reference to a record can help. Tell us who or what it concerns and where the information came from. If you have a privately held document or information about a living relative, start with a description and check permission before sharing. [How to contribute](CONTRIBUTING.md)
