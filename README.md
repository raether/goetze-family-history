*A shared, source-critical family history across Germany and the United States.*

## The story we are reconstructing

Our focus is the Götze / Goetze family, its links to the Römmler / Roemmler and Ulbrich families, and its movements between **Glauchau, Frankfurt, Leipzig, Berlin, Hesse, and the United States**.

In 1856, *Götze senior und Söhne* operated a textile business in Glauchau, Saxony. A later physician, **Rudolf Emil Fritz Götze (1863–1920)**, came from a family described as connected to the weaving trade. **A newly examined original marriage register from Dresden (8 April 1892, No. 228) now identifies his parents directly as Emil Hermann Götze and Albertine, née Brunner.** It also identifies his wife Else Römmler and her photographer father Carl Emil Römmler. [Read the evidence summary](records/marriages/1892-dresden-goetze-roemmler.md).

Our work also documents difficult history. Rudolf Götze was publicly involved in an **antisemitic campaign in 1893–1894** against the Jewish Schmul family's legally approved use of the surname Goetze. We report this directly, using contemporary newspapers and historical scholarship; we do not attribute ancestral beliefs or actions to descendants.

Research into later generations includes a promising **California lead for Gutrune Goetze / Falckon**, and archival material about **Gertraute Goetze-Fischer** in New York. Family connections must be confirmed by records, not just matching names.

## Join the research

If you are one of our American cousins, your family papers and recollections may hold exactly the evidence that German archives lack: names written on photographs, dates of immigration, stories of correspondence, letters, and spelling changes.

You do **not** need to be a historian or use Git. Read [How to contribute](CONTRIBUTING.md). You can [open a research issue](https://github.com/Contexter/goetze-family-history/issues/new/choose), or share a lead in our existing family Facebook conversation and let us know whether it may be added here.

For every contribution, please distinguish **what you witnessed or were told** from **what a dated document establishes**. Living relatives' private details, unpublished letters, and photographs are not published without permission.

## How to read this archive

- **Original record:** a contemporary document or an accessible transcription of it. We say explicitly when the scan has not been viewed.
- **Historical scholarship:** a documented interpretation by a researcher.
- **Family memory:** valuable testimony, but not an independently confirmed event.
- **Open question:** a lead awaiting verification.

This is a **curated public edition**, not a mirror of the private working archive. The private archive and its complete Git history contain research correspondence, personal recollections, and unresolved claims and are not exposed here. Source citations appear in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). No private photographs or third-party archival material are reproduced.

_This archive is a work in progress. Corrections, especially well-sourced corrections, are welcome._
