# Gutrune Falckon’s Goetze–Roemmler family chronicle (1988)

- **Source type:** Family memoir and genealogical notes
- **Author:** Gutrune Falckon, née Goetze
- **Title on the manuscript:** *Chronik der Familien Goetze – Roemmler*
- **Extent:** 37 numbered pages in the family copy, chiefly typescript with handwritten annotations

Gutrune dated her opening October 1988 and wrote in a later passage that it was the end of November 1988. A handwritten note on the first page says that Dieter Goetze, Albrecht’s son, received the chronicle on 8 March 1989.

The chronicle combines Gutrune’s own memories, stories told by relatives, and information she obtained from the family tree. It is especially detailed about the household of Rudolf Goetze and Else Roemmler, their children, and Gutrune’s own life in Germany and abroad. Her account names Gertraute as her sister and Gunter as her twin brother. The 1938 passenger manifest separately names a sister recorded as G. Fischer and a brother recorded as H. Goetze.

## Page guide

| Manuscript pages | Subjects |
| --- | --- |
| 1–5 | Early Goetze and Brunner names, the Glauchau textile business, and the family of Emil Hermann Goetze and Friederike Brunner |
| 6–9 | Rudolf, the Naunhof sanatorium, the move to Hesse, and the opening of the Roemmler history |
| 9–17 | The Roemmler, Arndt, and Heinigke families, and the two Goetze–Roemmler households |
| 18–30 | Hildegard, Erna and Brigitte, then Hermann, Gertraute, Albrecht, Gottfried, and Raimund |
| 31–37 | Gutrune’s life, closing research notes, and branches of the family abroad |

The family archive holds the German scans, an annotated English translation, and a continuous English reading edition. The page citations in the profiles use the numbered pages of the German manuscript.

## How the biographies cite it

A statement attributed to the chronicle records what Gutrune wrote. Civil or immigration records are cited alongside it where available. For example, the chronicle gives Rudolf and Else’s marriage as 9 April 1892. The [Dresden civil marriage register summary](../marriages/1892-dresden-goetze-roemmler.md) records 8 April 1892.
