# An original marriage record links the Glauchau and Dresden families (1892)

[Rudolf Emil Fritz Götze](../../people/goetze/rudolf-goetze.md) · [Else Römmler](../../people/roemmler/else-roemmler.md)

**Dresden civil marriage register, entry No. 228, 8 April 1892.** The two photographed pages were provided by a family researcher on **22 November 2026** and examined directly. The photos are **not included** in this public repository because their reproduction rights and original archive attribution have not yet been established. This is an evidence-based summary, not a public facsimile.

## What the civil record actually says

| Person | Recorded information |
| --- | --- |
| **Dr. med. Rudolf Emil Fritz Götze** | Born **23 September 1863** in **Glauchau**; resident in **Würzburg** in 1892 |
| **His father** | **Emil Hermann Götze**, merchant |
| **His mother** | **Albertine Caroline, née Brunner**; the complete sequence of given names is not yet confidently read from the small image |
| **Else Römmler** | Born **7 May 1868** in **Dresden**; resident in Dresden in 1892; the document spells her name **Else** |
| **Her father** | **Carl Emil Römmler**, photographer |
| **Her mother** | **Johanne**, maiden surname not yet securely deciphered |
| **Marriage** | **8 April 1892**, Dresden, **register No. 228** |
| **Witnesses** | Both fathers: **Carl Emil Römmler**, aged **50**, and **Emil Hermann Götze**, aged **64**. Each signs the register |

Both fathers, and the parents of the couple, are described as living in Dresden in 1892. The precise street names on the supplied photographs need a clearer image to establish.

## What this resolves

The marriage record is an **original, individual civil-register source** for the parentage of Rudolf Götze. It confirms the previously tentative identification of his parents as **Emil Hermann Götze and Albertine née Brunner**; the separate 1857 Frankfurt marriage record cited in the *Hessische Biografie* independently associates that couple with the Glauchau entrepreneur.

It also **corrects a date**: Else was born on **7 May 1868**, **not 7 April 1868** as an older family-research note stated. The older date should not be repeated.

## What is still open

Rudolf's **1863 baptism entry** may still tell us his godparents and his family's address at the time. A clearer image of the 1892 civil record is needed to confirm the remaining difficult handwriting, especially the full given names of his mother, Else's mother's maiden surname, and some historical street addresses.
