# Gutrune Götze — immigration and marriage records

Contributed by Helmut Raether. These are historical source documents and associated research notes; uncertain readings are retained rather than silently corrected.

## 1938 arrival in New York

- [Passenger manifest summary](1938%20Passinger%20Manifest.md)
- Original manifest scans: [page 1](Passenger%20Manifest%20Page%201.jpg), [page 2](Passenger%20Manifest%20Page%202.jpg), [page 3](Passenger%20Manifest%20Page%203.jpg)
- [Source citation](Source%20for%20Passenger%20Manifest.png)
- National Archives and Records Administration (NARA), New York passenger and crew lists, microfilm T715, Record Group 85, list 4, line 3; copy via Ancestry.
- Arrival: 13 July 1938, New York, aboard the S.S. Statendam from Rotterdam. The manifest names a brother H. Goetze in Berlin and a sister G. Fischer in New York. Their given names and the exact address readings must be reconciled with other evidence.

## 1938 declaration of intention

- [Original declaration](Declaration%20of%20Intention.jpg), signed 14 October 1938, Southern District of New York.
- Records the name Gutrune Goetze, birth on 8 June 1903 in Naunhof, last foreign residence Berlin, occupation masseuse, and arrival on 13 July 1938.
- This document establishes a declaration of intention, not completed naturalization.
- [Source citation](Source%20for%20Declaration%20of%20Intention.png)
- Courtesy of the National Archives at Philadelphia, Declarations of Intention for Citizenship, NAI 4713410, Record Group 21; copy via Ancestry.
