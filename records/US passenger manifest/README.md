These two scans have matching page structure and appear to belong to the same U.S. immigration passenger-list sheet, around **1938**, but the names, voyage, arrival date and identifying archival citation are **not yet securely read**. No proposed link to an individual family member should be presented as fact until corroborated.

Contributed on 23 November 2026. Both original JPEGs are preserved byte-for-byte.
